Auction 22 October 2026

On: Oct 16Author: B.J CroceCategories: Home, News, online auctions, Weekly Auction

We had a great auction last week with the Aboriginal art, gold & silver items, pair of vintage Chinese gilded petal nowls, two vintage Japanese Bronze thatched roof house incense burners, bronze thatched roof house, large group vintage stereoscope slides, lot of vintage military equipment & items, A L Gatliff gilt framed oil painting on canvas, boxes of mixed video game & card game items & lots more were very sought after.

We have been hard at work on getting this weeks auction with goods such as australian pottery, vinyl lp records, c.1960/70s ladies two piece bright Yellow Terry Towling bikini set, vintage wooden barley twist pedestals, hardcover book Old Vintage Melbourne 1960-1990, vintage stool with concave wooden seat & tubular metal legs, art glass items & works of art including ; Hal Waugh (1860 – 1941), James Matthews (British, active c.1880 – 1920), Natasha Florean (1950 – ), Douglas George Bell (Active c.1880 – 1900), Ian Laurence Stansfield (1954 – ), Robert Kerr (1925 – 2003), Wendy Stokes (1957 – ), Geoffrey Sparks (Active c.1980s), John William Roach (1894 – 1973) & more to come!

 

 

Auction will be live late Wednesday with viewing in our rooms Wed & Thurs 3-6 pm.

VIEW LIVE & PAST AUCTIONS HERE

Mon 10am-6pm

Tues 10am-6pm

Wed 10am-4pm

Thurs 3pm-6pm

Frid 10am-6pm
