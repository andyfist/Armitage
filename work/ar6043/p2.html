Search Words

The Auction Rooms advises auction clients of the benefits of using both broad and specific terms when searching for items of interest. To enter your search words, login and enter them in your account.

Your Search Keywords

These words and phrases will be used to help you find lots with items you are interested in.

The “View My Lots” feature, available when viewing an auction, allows you to refine your search and see only the most relevant results.

The words and phrases should be separated by commas. The more specific your terms, the more targeted your results will be. However, using general terms can help you find a wider range of items, which may be useful at times.

Note:

Part-matches are displayed to maximize results, so you may occasionally see unexpected items listed. For example, if you list “wood” as one of your search words, lots with “Wedgwood” or “wooden” in their descriptions will also be displayed.

If you need assistance with this feature, please contact The Auction Rooms.
