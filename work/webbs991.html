Webb's (Auckland) Fine Wine & Whiskies Monthly October Online, auction 991. Closes from 10:30 PM NZDT Wednesday 14 Oct 2026 (8:30 PM Hobart). Prices in NZD.

All 562 whisky and spirits lots were scanned with every photo read at the largest size Webb's serves (1,000 px), every label cropped and enlarged. Webb's descriptions held up: 299 lots scored 0 and only 20 reached 3 or more. Opus re-read the photos and checked every comp for those 20 and confirmed no strongly hidden-value bottle. The real title errors are small: lot 1309 is a Royal Salute 21 Sapphire Flagon listed as Chivas Regal 21 (keep max NZ$190, bid NZ$120 when checked), lot 1009 is single grain listed as single malt, lot 945 has the wrong bottling year, and lot 1384 claims 'single cask' with nothing on the label to back it. The standout price gap is not a labelling one: lot 1375, a 1960s White Horse Cellar Old Blend, was at NZ$240 when checked against one same-label sale of about NZ$1,360 (keep max NZ$1,110, Medium confidence, one comp only). Lots 1332 and 1333 (JW Blue Legendary Eight) have a keep max of NZ$465 against a NZ$400 opening bid. The valuable bottles (Samaroli Tomintoul 1234, Glenfarclas 1962 945, Ardbeg Rare Cask 1019) open at or near market value, so after the 22.4% premium there is no margin.

How this was done

  • Photos: every image for each lot was downloaded at the largest size Webb's serves (the _xl files, about 1,000 px on the long side; no larger version exists). Scanners read each full-size photo and cropped and enlarged label areas 3x.
  • Scan by Sonnet (16 in parallel), comps by Sonnet, every flagged lot re-checked by Opus, which re-read the photos and opened every comp, dropping any that did not match.
  • Lots scoring 3 or more on hidden value (20 lots) went to comps. Scores measure how likely the photos show value the title misses, not how good the whisky is.
  • Buyer's premium is 19.5% of hammer plus 15% GST: all-in = hammer x 1.22425. Maximum bids are hammer prices in NZD and use SOLD results only, from the last 24 months, same bottling.
  • UK auction results were converted from GBP to NZD at the rates stated on each card. Where a comp's buyer's premium status is known it is stated.
  • Freight, duty and GST to bring spirits from New Zealand into Australia are not included and can be large relative to cheaper bottles.
  • Current bids and opening bids were read from Webb's public lot pages on 13 Oct and will have changed. Nothing was bid, watched or registered.
