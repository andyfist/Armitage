## How to read the price columns
- **Only SOLD rows should set bids.** ASKING and UNSOLD rows are context.
- **Hammer or premium:** the hammer column says what the source page said. Many Australian house pages (Leski, Lawsons, Theodore Bruce, Leonard Joel, Gibson's, Davidson) don't say. Where a row says "not stated", treat the price as possibly including premium.
- **Invaluable prices come in three kinds:**
  - **"Invaluable displayed sold price (signed in)":** read on screen on Andy's free account.
  - **"read from Invaluable page code (not displayed on free account)":** the free account shows "Upgrade subscription to view". The figure was taken from the lot page's underlying data. Wherever both were available they matched, so treat these as reliable, with the premium basis not stated.
  - **"hidden":** not read at all.
- **eBay rows:**
  - "best offer accepted" means the listed price is shown and the actual sale price was lower.
  - Postage is extra and noted.
- **Other links:** Davidson rows link to the Davidson home page because its archive search gives no per-lot URL. Lot, sale and date are in the row.
- **Dates:** in mixed formats (ISO and "4 Dec 2022"). Normalise if needed.

### Lot 39: Robbery Under Arms, Remington 1888, 3 vols
SOLD, same edition, 2016 on:
- A$2,200, Sydney Rare Book Auctions, May 2019. Half leather with repairs, estimate A$3,000 to 3,600.
- US$2,400 incl. premium, Sotheby's NY, Jun 2024. The Bradley Martin copy, original cloth, half-titles present, "fine".
- A$6,500 hammer, ABA, May 2024. Later calf, slipcase.

The ABA result is the high outlier, and an original-cloth copy in better condition than Andy's made about A$3,700 all-in. Pre-2016 context: Sotheby's London, 2013, original cloth sold together with The Miner's Right, GBP 1,875 incl. premium. Dealers ask A$2,800 (rebound) to A$10,500 (original cloth, recased).

ABA lot 97 on Invaluable (https://www.invaluable.com/auction-lot/browne-thomas-alexander-rolf-bolderwood-robbery-u-97-c-9924411a08) has one photo only: the calf spines in the slipcase, with vol III labelled "BOLDERWOOD". There are no cloth or title-page photos to compare.

Identification points from the sold copies:
- "Bolderwood" misspelt on the title pages and spines.
- Green cloth blocked in black with a floral and butterfly design.
- Patterned (floral) endpapers.
- A half-title in each volume.

Andy's photo 02 seems to go straight from the front pastedown (with a pasted portrait clipping) to Chapter I. So vol I may lack its half-title and title; check for stubs.

### Lot 3: Malot, Sans famille and En famille
SOLD: one 1878 Dentu first, EUR 5,040 "price realised" (incl. premium), Christie's Paris online, 5 Jul 2023 (estimate EUR 4,000 to 6,000). It was in contemporary brown half chagrin, 174 x 111 mm. The price was read on Christie's sold-lots search. A Holland-paper first passed at Ader in March 2025. There are no visible SOLD prices for later 2-vol printings or for En famille, and dealers ask EUR 30 to 200 a set.

Andy's four volumes are bound uniformly with a 2-vol En famille, which BnF dates to 1895 or later. So the Sans famille is most likely a later printing.

Title page checks:
- An edition statement ("dixième édition" 1879, "31e" 1882, "47e" 1885) or "Ouvrage couronné par l'Académie française" means a later printing. The Prix Montyon was 1879.
- Collation does NOT prove a first. The 1878 first has 345 + 420 pp, an 8 pp Dentu catalogue at the end of vol I and 4 pp of music in vol II, but the 1879 printing matches.
- The 1888 Dentu reset is 395 + 484 pp with a portrait.
- The En famille first is the 1893 illustrated large 8vo, not 2 vols.
