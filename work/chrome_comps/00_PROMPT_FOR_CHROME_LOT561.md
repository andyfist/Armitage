Lot 561 is "9 boxes of art reference books" at Armitage Auctions, Launceston (22% buyer's premium). I photographed every box at viewing (folder `photos`, `viewing_box01` to `viewing_box10`). The auction's own 8 photos are in `auction_listing_8photos.jpg`.

**Your job:** find out whether ANY single book in these boxes is worth more than about A$80. Most of them will be common. I want the few exceptions, with real sold prices.

## Rules
- **Read only.** Never bid, buy, make offers, watch, register, sign in, accept terms, message anyone, or submit any form other than a site's own search box.
- You may read pages on sites where I'm already signed in, but take no action there.
- **Paywalls:** if a price is hidden on screen (for example Invaluable's "Upgrade to view"), you MAY read it from the page code. Label that row "page code behind paywall".
- **Only report prices you actually found,** with a URL and a date. Keep the original currency, and say hammer or incl. premium if the page says. Put SOLD, UNSOLD and ASKING in separate rows. Never estimate or invent a price. If you find nothing, write "no comps".
- **Comp window:** 2016 onwards.
- Plain Australian English, no em-dashes.

## How to work (fast triage, then depth)
1. **Triage every title in about 30 seconds.** Run an AbeBooks search sorted by price, plus an eBay sold search (eBay.com.au and eBay.com, "Sold items"). If the cheapest copies on AbeBooks are under about A$40 and eBay sold prices are under about A$40, mark it "common, under A$40" and move on. One row is enough.
2. **Go deeper only on titles that look like more than about A$80.** Check LiveAuctioneers, Invaluable, the-saleroom, Australian Book Auctions price lists (australianbookauctions.com), Leski, Davidson, Lawsons, Leonard Joel, and Dominic Winter/Forum (UK).
3. **Edition matters.** Note which edition makes the money (for example "1st edition with dust jacket"). In the final summary, list what I should check on the copy itself, such as the copyright page, the jacket or a signature.
4. **Flag any of these straight away:** signed or inscribed copies; limited or numbered editions; Australian artist monographs from small presses (Craftsman House, Macmillan, Beagle Press, Lauraine Diggins); exhibition catalogues from the 1950s-70s; Folio Society limited editions (as opposed to the standard Folio titles); anything ex-library (this lowers value).
