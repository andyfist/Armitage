## Hits ($500 or more after costs)

### 1. Lot 895: 1942 £1 star note, Armitage/McFarlane. Closes 4:58pm
- [Lot page](https://armitage.bidsonline.com.au/catalogue_detail.aspx?cdid=3111157&chid=28975). Bid $0.
- Serial read from the photo as K/82 02122 with the star after both numbers (R.30s replacement).
- **Probably a note that has sold before.** The coin database (`databases/noble/`) has Noble
  sale 133 lot 3514, 24 Jul 2023: *K/42 02122\* (R.30s), flattened, four pin holes, otherwise
  fine*, **$1,300 hammer**. Same five-digit serial on a star note, and a 4 is easily read as an 8
  in a photo, so this is very likely the same note.
- **Value:** the pipeline's $2,150 median leans on a $3,800 note (Noble 142, K/75 03355\*,
  *crisp original very fine*), a better note than this. If ours is the K/42 note, value it at
  its own last sale: $1,300.
- **Max bid: $1,330 on the pipeline's numbers; about $740 if it is the K/42 note** (same
  formula: $1,300 less 15% fees less $200, divided by 1.22).
- **Check:** the prefix (K/42 or K/82) and four pin holes. If both match, it is the 2023 Noble
  note. Also that the star is printed in both positions, and any tears, repairs or washing.

### 3. Lot 889: 1923 halfpenny, "6 clear pearls & centre diamond". Closes 4:56pm
- [Lot page](https://armitage.bidsonline.com.au/catalogue_detail.aspx?cdid=3111151&chid=28975). Bid $0.
- **Correction (7 Nov):** an earlier version of this report said the coin was set as jewellery.
  It is not. "Six pearls" and "centre diamond" are the crown details collectors use to grade and
  authenticate a genuine 1923; Noble describes its 1923s the same way. This is a loose coin, the
  key date of the Australian halfpenny series.
- **Value:** coin database, Noble, same description: $850 (sale 136, Jul 2024, *six pearls, part
  centre diamond, good very fine*), $750 and $800 (sale 135, Apr 2024, nearly very fine), $700
  (fine/good fine). Better coins with eight pearls made $2,400 (Jul 2026, extremely fine). eBay
  sold, 2025–26: about $520–1,040 including postage. Value for a six-pearl coin: about $750–950.
- **Max bid: $495.**
- **Check:** authenticity first: altered 1925/1928 dates and casts are common. Look at the 3 in
  the date, the peripheral die breaks either side, and the weight (about 5.7 g).
