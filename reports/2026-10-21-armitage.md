## Hits

### 1. Lot 883: Tudor Oyster gents watch. Closes 5:24pm
- [Lot page](https://armitage.bidsonline.com.au/catalogue_detail.aspx?cdid=3088327&chid=28839). Bid $440 (30 bids). **House estimate $200–300.**
- **Photos:** 1950s Tudor Oyster with a "honeycomb" (waffle) textured dial, applied gold
  markers, "Shock-Resisting", screw-down Oyster crown and smooth bezel, with a plain caseback
  showing a reference number. The honeycomb dial is the collectable feature.
- **Value:** Dealer prices for honeycomb-dial Tudor Oysters (ref 7934 and similar) run about
  US$3,200–4,850. A conservative quick-sale value is **about A$3,500–4,500**.
- **Max bid: about $2,000.**
- **Risks:** a refinished or redialled dial, an aftermarket crown or hands, a heavily polished
  case, or a service history that's unknown. Ask Armitage for the reference and serial (inside
  the caseback and between the lugs) and for a photo of the movement. "Working" is all the
  listing says.

### 2. Lot 818: Dior men's Oblique Saddle bag (boxed) + Prada Saffiano belt bag. Closes 5:02pm
- [Lot page](https://armitage.bidsonline.com.au/catalogue_detail.aspx?cdid=3088262&chid=28839). Bid $405. **House estimate $60–100.**
- **Photos:** black Oblique jacquard men's Saddle bag in a Dior box with dust bag, branded
  strap and a care card. The Prada is a Saffiano leather belt or crossbody bag with a gold
  triangle logo and a chain strap.
- **Value:** pre-owned men's Dior Saddles sell for about €1,200–2,400 (an Oblique Saddle sold
  for US$1,600 on eBay). The Prada adds about A$300–500. Total **about A$2,400–4,000**.
- **Max bid: about $1,100.**
- **Risks:** authenticity. Fakes of both are common. Inspect at viewing: the Dior serial or
  date tag inside, stitching and hardware engraving. Sell through an authenticated reseller.

### 3. Lot 943: Warburton *Journey Across the Western Interior of Australia* (1875) + 2 Tasmanian AIF histories. Closes 5:43pm
- [Lot page](https://armitage.bidsonline.com.au/catalogue_detail.aspx?cdid=3088384&chid=28839). Bid $135 (26 bids). **House estimate $30–40.**
- **Photos:** Warburton in the first-edition green pictorial cloth with gilt camels on the
  front ("Across Australia"), inscribed 1898. Also *The Fortieth* (40th Battalion AIF, Hobart
  1922, foreword by Monash, with a period Launceston owner's inscription) and *The Story of the
  Twelfth* (12th Battalion, Walch, Hobart 1925). All three are originals, not reprints.
- **Value:** the Warburton first edition carried a £600–800 auction estimate (Bonhams 2017).
  The two Tasmanian battalion histories are scarce originals, about $200–400 each. Total
  **about A$1,800–2,500**, depending on whether the **folding map and 9 plates** are present.
- **Max bid: about $650.**
- **Risks:** a missing map or plates roughly halves the Warburton's value. Ask Armitage to
  confirm the map is present.
